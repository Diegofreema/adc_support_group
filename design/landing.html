Questions

Good to know

Does it cost anything?

No. Registering your group and adding members is free. Your group only pays when it chooses to support a campaign need.

Who can register a group?

Anyone leading a group that supports Atiku Abubakar: a women's group, a youth club, a market association, a church or mosque group, a diaspora network. You become the group leader.

How do people join my group?

Send your join link on WhatsApp, let people scan your QR code at meetings, or upload your members from an Excel or CSV file. Each person gets their own account by SMS or email.

How does my group get verified?

After you register, the Campaign Directorate checks your details. This usually takes 1 to 3 days. You'll get an SMS or email when it's done.

I already belong to another group. Can I join this one too?

Yes. Use one account for all your groups. You'll be counted once as a supporter, and each group sees you as their member.
